Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ c.privacy_updated }}

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. This policy explains which personal data we process when you visit this website or contact us, why we do it, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG).

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website is:

{{ owner_block() }}
{{ contact_block() }}

Full details are in our Imprint. We are not required to appoint a data protection officer.

2. Overview

  • We do not use Google Analytics or any other tracking or advertising service.
  • We do not use social media plugins, external fonts or other third-party content: all fonts and files are served from our own server.
  • We use only one technically necessary cookie and no tracking cookies, so no cookie banner is needed.
  • We do not sell your data and do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted on a server operated by:
{{ c.legal_host | replace('\n', ', ') }}

We have concluded a data processing agreement with the hosting provider (Art. 28 GDPR). The server is located in the European Union.

Whenever you open a page, your browser automatically sends information that our web server stores in log files: your full IP address, date and time, the requested page, the amount of data transferred, the referring page, and your browser and operating system (user agent).

We need this data to deliver the website and to keep it secure, for example to detect and block attacks (DDoS, brute force). The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and working website (Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR). Log files are deleted automatically after 14 days. IP addresses that attack the server may be blocked temporarily by our firewall.

4. Visitor statistics (without cookies)

To understand how often our website is visited, we keep simple statistics on our own server. No third party is involved and no cookies are used. We store:

  • a shortened (anonymised) IP address – the last part is removed before saving (e.g. 203.0.113.0), so it can no longer identify you;
  • the page visited, date and time, the referring page and your browser type.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding and improving our website (Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR). The data is deleted automatically after {{ visit_days }} days.

5. Cookies

We use a single session cookie (__Host-amxme_session). It contains a random security token that protects our contact form against misuse (CSRF protection) and, if you received a preview link, your temporary preview access. It is deleted when you close your browser and is not used for tracking.

Because this cookie is strictly necessary to provide the website you requested, it does not require consent (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). The legal basis for the related processing is Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR.

6. Contact form and email

When you send us a message using the contact form or by email, we process the data you provide: your name, email address, the service you are interested in and your message. We also store the IP address from which the form was sent, only to prevent spam and abuse; it is deleted after {{ message_ip_days }} days.

We use this data only to answer your inquiry and, if applicable, to prepare an offer or contract. After you send the form you receive an automatic confirmation email.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR if your inquiry relates to a contract or pre-contractual steps, and otherwise our legitimate interest in answering inquiries (Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR). Providing the data is voluntary, but we cannot answer you without an email address.
